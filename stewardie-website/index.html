Who's taking out the recycling? Did anyone feed the cat?

Most shared organizers feel either like cold enterprise ticketing systems or chaotic messaging group chats where tasks get buried. Stewardie changes that dynamic completely.

The "Today" Flow

Zero-friction responsibility, no micromanagement. Tasks flow naturally through clear states: Unclaimed, Requested, Covered ("I've got it!"), and Done.

Moments Camera

Dedicated in-app camera. Attach completion photos to tasks or post standalone candid moments. Share quick memories and leave grateful heart reactions.

Daily Mood Check-Ins

Low-pressure daily check-ins pairing a mood status with customized pastel clay colors (Sky, Butter, Rose). Quickly signal teammates if you could use a hand.

Smart Calendar & Agenda

Unified chronological timeline with person-aware filtering. Toggle between Shared, Your, or specific co-members without switching identities.

Shared Spaces & Dynamic Memberships

Create dedicated spaces for your Family, Housemates, Friends, or Crew. Join via 6-character invite codes, universal links, or QR code scanning with a built-in mobile scanner. Privacy-first by default with private cloud storage and granular member controls.
